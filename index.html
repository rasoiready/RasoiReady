Our Story & Vision

"I am Bhavesh, a proud son of a farmer from Jalgaon (425001). After facing losses in our traditional vegetable retail stall, I realized that modern households don't just need vegetables—they need convenience without compromising on farm-fresh quality."

For all Jalgaonkars, cleaning crabs is a hassle—we solve this by delivering perfectly cleaned and boiled crabs directly to your doorstep.
